# Changelog

## 0.1.1

- Added real-time steering, automatic fire, moving fleets, and traveling projectiles with collisions.
- Added four arcade waves, including a flagship encounter, with a brief break between waves.
- Pause flight when the Kindle sleeps or returns from USB drive mode. Save and resume the mission and best score from a separate arcade save.

## 0.1.0

- Added three short waves and a flagship encounter with lane changes, targeted shots, and limited shields.
- Saved the active mission and best score for offline play.
